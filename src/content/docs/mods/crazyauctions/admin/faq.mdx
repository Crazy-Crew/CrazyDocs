    <TabCard label="#1">
        #### Why do some colors look weird?
        - Add `-Dnet.kyori.ansi.colorLevel=truecolor` to your startup flags!
    </TabCard>
    <TabCard label="#2">
        #### Why can't my players use /ah?
        - CrazyAuctions requires **Vault** and an economy plugin that hooks into Vault. Without Vault the plugin will not load.
        - Check that players have `crazyauctions.access` (and `crazyauctions.sell` / `crazyauctions.bid` to list items). All of these are true by default.
        - `crazyauctions.sell.#` / `crazyauctions.bid.#` are a listing **cap**, not an access permission. See [Commands & Permissions](/mods/crazyauctions/reference/commands/).
    </TabCard>
    <TabCard label="#3">
        #### How do I limit how many items a player can list?
        - Give them `crazyauctions.sell.#` and/or `crazyauctions.bid.#`, replacing `#` with a number.
        - The cap counts all of the player's current listings (selling and bidding combined), and the highest number wins.
        - With no such node, listings are unlimited. `crazyauctions.bypass` skips the cap.
    </TabCard>
    <TabCard label="#4">
        #### How does tax work?
        - `Settings.Percent-Tax` in `config.yml` is the percent taken from the seller's earnings. Use a whole number, such as `5` for 5%.
        - Tax currently only applies to buy-now (selling) sales, not to won bids.
        - `Settings.Auction-List-Fee` is a flat amount the player pays every time they list an item with `/ca sell` or `/ca bid`.
    </TabCard>
    <TabCard label="#5">
        #### Can I use MySQL or SQLite for storage?
        - No. Storage is YAML only, and all listings are saved in `data.yml`.
    </TabCard>
    <TabCard label="#6">
        #### Are there any PlaceholderAPI placeholders?
        - No. CrazyAuctions does not provide a PlaceholderAPI expansion. PlaceholderAPI is only used to parse placeholders in GUI item names and lore.
    </TabCard>
