    <TabCard label="#1">
        #### Why do some colors look weird?
        - Add `-Dnet.kyori.ansi.colorLevel=truecolor` to your startup flags!
    </TabCard>
    <TabCard label="#2">
        #### Why can't my players use /ah?
        - CrazyAuctions requires **Vault** and an economy plugin that hooks into Vault. Without Vault the plugin will not load.
        - Check that players have `crazyauctions.access` (and `crazyauctions.sell` / `crazyauctions.bid` to list items). All of these are true by default.
        - To list items, players also need `crazyauctions.sell.#` / `crazyauctions.bid.#` with a number, because without one their listing limit is 0. See [Commands & Permissions](/mods/crazyauctions/reference/commands/).
    </TabCard>
    <TabCard label="#3">
        #### How do I limit how many items a player can list?
        - Give them `crazyauctions.sell.#` and/or `crazyauctions.bid.#`, replacing `#` with a number.
        - `sell.#` counts only the player's selling listings and `bid.#` counts only their bidding listings. If a player has more than one node of the same kind, the highest number wins.
        - With no such node, the limit is 0 and the player cannot list. `crazyauctions.bypass` skips the limit.
    </TabCard>
    <TabCard label="#4">
        #### Can I use MySQL or SQLite for storage?
        - No. Storage is YAML only, and all listings are saved in `data.yml`.
    </TabCard>
    <TabCard label="#5">
        #### Are there any PlaceholderAPI placeholders?
        - No. CrazyAuctions does not provide its own PlaceholderAPI expansion. When PlaceholderAPI is installed, placeholders in single-line `messages.yml` messages sent to players are parsed. GUI item names and lore are not run through PlaceholderAPI.
    </TabCard>
