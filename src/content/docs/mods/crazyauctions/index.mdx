## Requirements
:::tip
CrazyAuctions requires you to use at least **Paper 26.X.X** or a fork of and **Java 25** to run.

It also requires [Vault](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/vault.34315/) and an economy plugin that hooks into Vault.
:::
