## Custom Heads
| Plugin       | Link                                                                | Required | Deprecated | Removed | Added | Planned |

| HeadDatabase | [Download](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/head-database.14280/) | no       | no         | no      | yes   | n/a     |

HeadDatabase is used for custom head fountains. The head IDs for each fountain are listed under `settings.heads` in `config.yml`.
